About these reports

Where do the reports come from?

From collectors like you. Anyone can report what they got at a McDonald's: no account and no sign-in. To remove a report, email the address below.

Why does one store only have a few of the toys?

Happy Meal toys ship to restaurants in cases that each hold a few of the toys in a series. A store hands out whatever case is open, so two McDonald's a mile apart can have different toys the same week. Ask at the counter: many crews will swap if they have another case open.

What is sent when I report?

Only the restaurant (its map ID, name, address and location), the series and the toys you ticked, plus the time. Nothing about you, your device or your own location is sent. Store locations come from OpenStreetMap. Place searches go to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim; "Near me" stays in your browser.

A report looks wrong or a store is missing

Email toyticker@gmail.com and the report will be removed. Missing restaurants can be added on OpenStreetMap; they show up here after the next monthly refresh.
